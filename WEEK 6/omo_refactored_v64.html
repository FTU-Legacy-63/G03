OPERATING MANUAL · OMO

Guide

1 · Observe. Read the three-page OMO Daily: macro context, the interbank rate, and the live outstanding/maturity file.
2 · Decide. Choose the OMO instrument, auction method, offered volume, and the applicable rate/pricing method. Phase 1 also asks for expected inflation for the three-phase macro block.
3 · Process bids. Five bank bids arrive from the engine. Sort them by the rule for the selected auction method, then approve the auction record.
4 · Review transmission. Results show awarded volume, liquidity, the updated interbank rate, outstanding positions, maturity cash flows, and—when available—the macro block.
