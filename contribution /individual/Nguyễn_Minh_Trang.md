### **6\. Remaining Limitation**

One key limitation is that some market indicators and policy-related concepts may not be immediately clear to users who have limited knowledge of monetary policy. Further usability testing would help identify confusing elements, improve the explanations of key indicators, and ensure that the interface communicates the consequences of players’ decisions more clearly.

Although the game already includes interactive controls, decision-making stages, and simulation results, there is still room to expand player engagement such as having more dynamic responses to players’ decisions.
