## **How My Work Is Used**

* **Economic model development:** Connects the player's decisions with auction outcomes, estimated interbank interest rates and its consequences on GDP growth and inflation. The formulas and parameter choices also make the transmission from the money market to the broader economy more explicit.  
* **Learning card development:** Helps players interpret the underlying concepts, improving learning experience.  
* **Backend testing:** Checks whether the implemented rules and simulation outcomes are consistent with the intended logic across different decisions and liquidity conditions.  
* **Frontend support and API integration:** Connects the user interface with the backend through API, enabling players’ actions to be processed and simulation results to be displayed in the game.

## **Integration, Testing and Revision**

I worked across the economic-model, frontend and backend-testing components. I supported the integration of economic calculations into the simulation, checked the consistency between model assumptions and implemented rules, and tested different decisions under different liquidity conditions.

During development, some formulas and coefficients were revised to make the transmission from money-market conditions to macroeconomic outcomes more visible in the simulation. These revisions were intended to improve the educational value of the game, rather than to claim that every relationship precisely reproduces real-world effects.

## **Remaining Limitation**

A key limitation is that the economic model is not yet fully empirically validated. Although formula research, historical data collection and regression analysis informed its development, some relationships rely on economic intuition, and some formulas and coefficients were adjusted to make the intended transmission mechanism more visible. 

Consequently, the model is better understood as a simplified educational simulation than as a validated tool for forecasting actual macroeconomic outcomes.
