# Data

## OMO Game — Database

The dataset used to develop and calibrate the Open Market Operations (OMO) simulation is available here:

**[DATABASE 2.0 — Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1GDpEZRkXhVWKvsum6EEYFXGNjwku5HiF/edit?gid=520085657#gid=520085657)**

The workbook contains reference data on interbank and policy interest rates, inflation, GDP growth, historical OMO auctions, SBV liquidity injections and withdrawals, and other macroeconomic and banking indicators. The **Source** worksheet documents the data references.
